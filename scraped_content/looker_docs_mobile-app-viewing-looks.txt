If access to the Looker mobile app is enabled for your instance, you can view [Looks](/looker/docs/saving-and-editing-looks) and [dashboards](/looker/docs/mobile-app-viewing-dashboards) on your mobile device in the Looker mobile app.

While viewing a Look in the app, you can switch between portrait mode and landscape mode, and select the options to add or remove the Look from your favorites, share a link to the Look, copy the link to the Look to your clipboard, and view information about the Look from the Look's three-dot menu.

## Opening a Look

You can navigate to Looks from the [**Recently Viewed** and **Favorites** screens](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#viewing_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content), while [viewing a board](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#navigating_to_content_from_boards) that you follow, or while [browsing content in folders](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#browsing_content_in_folders) in the Looker mobile app.

To open a Look in the app, select the [content card](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#content_cards) for the Look you want to view:

![Content card for the Weekly Returns Look shows a visual preview of the Look, the Look title, and the name of the Look creator.](/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-select-look-716.png)

You can also [search for a specific Look](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#searching_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content) on the **Recently Viewed** and **Favorites** screens.

## Viewing a Look

Looks in the Looker mobile app include the following elements:

![](/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-overview-716-v2.png)

  1. **Back button** : Returns you to the previous screen
  2. **Look title** : Displays the title of the Look
  3. **Three-dot menu**: Provides the options to add or remove the Look from your favorites, share the Look, copy the link to the Look, or view information about the Look
  4. **Filters** : If an admin has enabled the [Show Filters on Embedded Looks](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-embed#show_filters_on_embedded_looks) option in the **Admin** panel, this section displays any [filters](/looker/docs/filtering-and-limiting) that have been applied to a Look

You can also tap a data point on the visualization to show a tooltip with more information about that data point.

![](/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-data-point-info-716.png)

## Switching between portrait mode and landscape mode

When you view a Look or [a dashboard](/looker/docs/mobile-app-viewing-dashboards) in the Looker mobile app, you can rotate the screen of your mobile device to switch between portrait mode and landscape mode.

The app displays content in portrait mode by default.

![](/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-portrait-716.png)

In landscape mode, the Look's visualization expands horizontally to fill the width of the screen.

![](/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-landscape-716.png)

## Using the three-dot menu for a Look

The Looker mobile app displays a three-dot menu at the top of a Look.

Select the three-dot menu for a Look to use the following options:

  * **Add to Favorites** or **Remove from Favorites**: Add a Look to your favorites, or remove a Look from your favorites.
  * **Share**: Share a link to a Look from your mobile device.
  * **Copy link**: Copy the link to a Look.
  * **Get info**: View information about a Look, including its title, information about views and favorites, the date the Look was created, and the name of the user who created the Look.

You can also access the three-dot menu for a Look from the [**Recently Viewed** and **Favorites** screens](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#viewing_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content), a [board](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#navigating_to_content_from_boards), or a [folder](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#browsing_content_in_folders) that contains the Look.

### Adding or removing a Look from your favorites

The [**Favorites** screen](/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#viewing_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content) in the Looker mobile app lists the Looks that you have added to your favorites.

To add a Look to your favorites while viewing the Look, or from the **Recently Viewed** screen, a board, or a folder:

  1. Select the three-dot menu for the Look.
  2. Choose **Add to Favorites**.

To remove a Look from your favorites while viewing the Look, or from the **Recently Viewed** screen, the **Favorites** screen, a board, or a folder:

  1. Select the three-dot menu for a Look that you have added to your favorites.
  2. Choose **Remove from Favorites**.

### Sharing a Look

You can share Looks with other users using the sharing options available on your mobile device, or with a third-party service that is [integrated with Looker](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-actions#list_of_integrated_services), such as [Slack](https://slack.com/).

### Copying the link to a Look

You can copy the link to a Look to your mobile device's clipboard:

  1. Select the three-dot menu for the Look.
  2. Select **Copy link**.

After you copy a link, the app displays the message **Link copied to clipboard**.

### Viewing information about a Look

Select **Get info** from the three-dot menu for a Look to view the following information:

  * **Title** : Shows the title of the Look, the number of views, and the number of favorites
  * **Description** : Displays the description of the Look, if a [description was added](/looker/docs/saving-and-editing-looks#editing_a_looks_description)
  * **Created** : Displays the date the Look was created and the name of the user who created it
