# https://cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-pages

Depth: 3

These pages appear under the **Platform** section of the Looker **Admin** panel:  
  
  * [Actions](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-actions): Configure the ability to deliver data to third-party services that are integrated with Looker.
  * [API](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-api): Change API settings in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Embed](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-embed): Change settings that are related to embedding in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Extension framework](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-extension-framework): Enable or disable the Looker [extension framework](/looker/docs/extension-framework) in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Gemini in Looker](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-gil): Enable or disable Gemini in Looker for Looker (original) instances. Verify Gemini in Looker enablement settings for Looker (Google Cloud core) instances.
  * [Marketplace](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-marketplace): Change settings that are related to the Looker [Marketplace](/looker/docs/marketplace) in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Themes](/looker/docs/themes-for-embedded-dashboards-and-explores): Customize the appearance of your embedded content from Looker.
  * [SMTP](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-smtp): Configure Looker email settings, including custom email settings.
  * [Visualizations](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-visualizations): View, add, edit, and delete custom visualization types.
  * [Looker Studio Pro](/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-lsp): Accept complimentary licenses to use Looker Studio Pro.
  * [Studio in Looker](/looker/docs/enabling-studio-in-looker): Enable or disable Studio in Looker for Looker (original) instances and for Looker (Google Cloud core) instances.
